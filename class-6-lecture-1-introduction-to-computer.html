Lecture 1: Introduction to Computer and its Major Parts

What is a computer? Meet its two major parts — hardware (the parts we can touch) and software (the instructions) — with everyday examples like the monitor, keyboard, Windows and Paint.

What you will learn

  • A computer is an electronic machine: it takes information from us, works on it very fast, and gives us the result.
  • We use computers for studying, drawing, playing games, watching videos and talking to friends.
  • Every computer has two major parts: hardware and software.
  • Hardware is the part we can touch — monitor, keyboard, mouse, system unit, printer and speakers.
  • Software is the part we cannot touch: the set of instructions that tells the computer what to do.
  • Examples of software: Windows (starts the computer), Microsoft Word (writing), Paint (drawing), games and the web browser.
  • Easy way to remember: hardware is like the body, and software is like the mind that runs it.

Full transcript

Assalam-o-Alaikum, dear students! Welcome to Computer Science, Class Six. I am your teacher, Hazrat Amin. Today we will learn: what is a computer, and what are its major parts?

A computer is an electronic machine. It takes information from us, works on it very fast, and gives us the result. We use computers for studying, drawing, playing games, watching videos, and talking to friends.

Every computer has two major parts: hardware and software. Hardware is the part that we can touch. Software is the part we cannot touch. It is the set of instructions that tells the computer what to do.

Now, examples of hardware. The monitor shows us pictures and words. The keyboard is used for typing. The mouse helps us point and click. The system unit is the brain box of the computer. The printer prints our work on paper, and the speakers give us sound.

Now, examples of software. Windows starts the computer. Microsoft Word helps us write. Paint helps us draw pictures. Games and the web browser are also software. Remember: hardware is like the body, and software is like the mind that runs it.

Let us revise. A computer is an electronic machine. Its two major parts are hardware, which we can touch, and software, which gives the instructions. In the next lesson, we will learn about input and output devices. Goodbye, and keep learning!
