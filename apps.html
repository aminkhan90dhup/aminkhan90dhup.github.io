Apps & AI Websites

The internet is full of powerful free tools — if you know where to look. On this page I have collected the best AI websites and useful apps that I personally recommend for students, teachers and creators. Almost all of them are free to start.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) websites can answer your questions in seconds, explain a difficult lesson in easy words, correct your English, translate languages, and even create pictures, videos and voices from a simple line of text. For a student, they are like a patient tutor available day and night. For a teacher, they help prepare lessons, notes and quizzes in minutes instead of hours. And for creators, they open a whole studio — design, editing and music — inside an ordinary mobile phone.

Start with one or two tools from the list below — ChatGPT or Gemini for questions, NotebookLM for your own books and notes, and Canva or CapCut for creative work. Every link opens the official website in a new tab.

⚠️ Use AI wisely: AI sometimes makes mistakes, so always double-check important answers with your book or teacher. Never share passwords, personal photos or private information on any AI website — and never pay for a plan when you are only learning; the free plans are more than enough.
