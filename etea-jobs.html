What is ETEA?

ETEA is the Educational Testing and Evaluation Agency of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. It runs written screening tests for government departments in the province — the courts, health, education, fisheries, local government and many more. When a department has vacant posts, it asks ETEA to advertise them, collect online applications and hold the test. That is why job seekers look for the latest ETEA advertisement every week: one new ETEA jobs advertisement can mean dozens of posts in a single project.

Each ETEA advertisement carries a project number — for example, ETEA Project 898 or Project 897. The project number is the easiest way to find the right advertisement on the ETEA website, because several ETEA projects are open at the same time. On this page you will find the ETEA latest advertisement and every ETEA project that is open right now, with its last date and a link to the full details.

To apply for ETEA jobs online, visit the official ETEA website (etea.edu.pk), create your profile, verify your CNIC at a NADRA E-Sahulat franchise, and fill in the online form for the project you want. Read the official advertisement first — it states the education, age limit, domicile and fee for each post. Never pay anyone who promises you a job; ETEA never asks for money outside its official fee channel.

How to apply online for ETEA jobs

ETEA jobs online apply follows the same steps for almost every project:

  1. Open the official ETEA website at etea.edu.pk and find the project by its number.
  2. Create your ETEA profile with your CNIC and a working mobile number.
  3. Verify your CNIC at a NADRA E-Sahulat franchise — the online form opens only after verification.
  4. Fill in the online form for the project, upload your photograph and documents if asked, and submit before the last date.
  5. Pay the test fee through the official channel given in the advertisement and keep the receipt safe.
  6. Download your roll number slip from the ETEA website a few days before the test, and take your original CNIC to the test centre.

ETEA jobs 2026 — frequently asked questions

What is ETEA?

ETEA is the Educational Testing and Evaluation Agency of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. It conducts recruitment tests for provincial government departments and announces new posts through its advertisements and projects.

How do I apply online for ETEA jobs?

Open the official ETEA website at etea.edu.pk, find your project by its number, create your profile, verify your CNIC at a NADRA E-Sahulat franchise, and submit the online form before the last date. The full steps are listed in the “How to apply online for ETEA jobs” section above.

What is the last date for ETEA jobs 2026?

Every project has its own last date — see the live list above. The nearest closing dates right now are 25 November 2026 (Project 886, Senior Civil Judge Karak) and 26 November 2026 (Project 887, District Health Office Kohistan Lower). Always apply a few days early, because the website can be slow on the last day.

What documents do I need for an ETEA application?

Your CNIC, a recent passport-size photograph, your domicile certificate, and your education certificates and degrees. Some posts also ask for experience certificates or a driving licence. Check the advertisement for the exact list for your post.

Where do I download the ETEA roll number slip?

Roll number slips are published on the official ETEA website a few days before the test. Open etea.edu.pk, go to the roll number slip section, enter your CNIC number and print your slip. Take the printed slip and your original CNIC to the test centre.

⚠️ Students, be careful: apply ONLY through the official ETEA website (etea.edu.pk). Real advertisements always state the post, education required and last date — read them fully before paying any test fee.
