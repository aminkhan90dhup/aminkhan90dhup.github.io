PCB confirms a packed home season: Sri Lanka and England in Pakistan

Pakistan will host Sri Lanka and England in a packed international schedule from 9 October to 21 November, the PCB has announced — three T20Is against Sri Lanka in Rawalpindi, a seven-match ODI tri-series in Rawalpindi and Lahore, and a two-Test series that is part of the ICC World Test Championship. Sri Lanka arrived on 4 October. Pakistan beat Sri Lanka by 37 runs in the ODI tri-series opener in Rawalpindi — Pakistan 305 all out in 49.5 overs (Salman Agha 107 off 90, Babar Azam 45, Saad Baig 39; Eshan Malinga 4/46) beat Sri Lanka 268 all out in 48.1 overs (Pavan Rathnayake 79, Janith Liyanage 60, Kamindu Mendis 44; Shaheen Afridi 3/55). Next is Pakistan vs England on 20 October in Rawalpindi. Pakistan then beat England by 87 runs on 20 October — Pakistan 302/9 in 50 overs (Hasan Nawaz 79 off 74, Arafat Minhas 59 off 58, Faheem Ashraf 39, Saad Baig 35, Babar Azam 34; Sonny Baker 3 wickets) beat England 215 all out in 38.4 overs (Tom Banton 57, Ben Duckett 45, Joe Root 33; Arafat Minhas 6/40, Haris Rauf 2 wickets). Sri Lanka then beat England by 2 wickets on 22 October — England 429/4 in 50 overs (Harry Brook 202 off 116, Joe Root 69, Will Jacks 66*, Tom Banton 50; Dunith Wellalage 2/56) lost to Sri Lanka 430/8 in 49.5 overs (Pavan Rathnayake 130 off 106, Avishka Fernando 97, Dasun Shanaka 97, Janith Liyanage 61*; Josh Tongue 3/79) in Rawalpindi — the second-highest successful chase in ODI history. Pakistan then beat England by 1 wicket on 25 October — England 241 all out (Ben Duckett 105, Sam Curran 42; Shaheen Afridi 4/46) lost to Pakistan 243/9 in 47.1 overs (Shadab Khan 113* off 96, Haris Rauf 7*; Jamie Overton 3/43) in Lahore — Shadab and Rauf added a record 89 for the 10th wicket after Pakistan were 154/9, booking Pakistan's place in the tri-series final. Sri Lanka then beat Pakistan by 8 wickets on 27 October — Pakistan 110 all out in 25.3 overs (Babar Azam 29; Dilshan Madushanka 5/34) lost to Sri Lanka 114/2 in 18.1 overs (Kamindu Mendis 62* off 60, Pavan Rathnayake 23*) in Lahore — Sri Lanka booked the tri-series final against Pakistan and England were eliminated. Next is Sri Lanka vs England on 29 October in Lahore.
