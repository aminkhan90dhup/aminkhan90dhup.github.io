Sri Lanka Women lift the Emerging Nations Trophy — beat Ireland by 6 wickets in the final

Unbeaten Sri Lanka Women clinched the ICC Women's Emerging Nations Trophy in Ajman on Wednesday 25 November, bowling Ireland out for 96 in 19.3 overs and chasing the target down in just 12.4 overs to win by six wickets. Sathya Sandeepani took 4/13 and was Player of the Final, while Imesha Dulani's unbeaten 44 off 25 balls sealed the chase at 99/4. Ireland, who lost captain Gaby Lewis in the opening over and slipped to 54/6, were held to 96 despite Alana Dalzell's 24. Sri Lanka won all eight matches in the tournament — succeeding Thailand, winners of the inaugural edition in 2025 — and opener Vishmi Gunaratne was Player of the Series with 324 runs.

Wednesday 25 November 2026 • Karwan Sports Club Cricket Ground, Ajman • Sources: ICC, Daily Mirror, Daily News, The Times of India
Cricket News

15-year-old Sooryavanshi smashes record 204 off 53 balls — fastest List A century and double century

India A's Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, 15, rewrote the List A record books in Doha on Tuesday 24 November, hammering 204 off just 53 balls against UAE in the Asia Cup Rising Stars — 28 sixes and seven fours at a strike rate of 384.90, the most sixes ever in a senior men's innings. He reached his century in 28 balls, beating Jake Fraser-McGurk's 29-ball record, his 150 in 40 balls, and his double century in 52 balls, smashing Chad Bowes' previous 103-ball mark. Chasing 250, India A reached 254/3 in only 14.2 overs to win by seven wickets; UAE had posted 249/8, led by Harpreet Singh's 130. India A finished second in Group B behind Pakistan Shaheens and meet Sri Lanka A in Thursday's first semi-final.

Tuesday 24 November 2026 • Doha • Sources: Reuters, ESPNcricinfo, Al Jazeera, The Times of India
Cricket News

New Zealand hold India to a draw in Wellington — 1st Test drawn, series 0–0

New Zealand batted out 120 overs on the final day to save the first Test against India at Basin Reserve, Wellington, on Monday 23 November. India, who declared on a mammoth 720/9 — their highest Test total in New Zealand, built on KL Rahul's 136 and Rishabh Pant's 128 — needed eight wickets on the last day to force a win, but Daryl Mitchell (49 not out off 186 balls) and Glenn Phillips (26 not out off 126) put on an unbeaten 63 for the sixth wicket and the captains shook hands with New Zealand on 239/5, still 113 behind. Devon Conway made 65 in the second innings after New Zealand's 368 first time out (Henry Nicholls 175 not out, Phillips 118). The draw leaves India fourth in the World Test Championship standings and New Zealand third; the second and deciding Test starts on Friday 27 November at Hagley Oval, Christchurch.

Monday 23 November 2026 • Basin Reserve, Wellington • Sources: ESPNcricinfo, ICC, Al Jazeera, The Indian Express
Cricket News

Pakistan beat Sri Lanka 1–0 to win the Test series — first series win of the WTC cycle

Pakistan wrapped up an eight-wicket win over Sri Lanka in the second Test in Rawalpindi on Friday 20 November to take the two-match series 1–0 — their first Test series win since beating England 2–1 at home in October 2024, and their first in the ICC World Test Championship 2025–27 cycle. The first Test at the same ground was drawn (Pakistan 434/9 declared and 284/8; Sri Lanka 446). In the second Test, Pakistan's pace attack bowled Sri Lanka out for 98 in 35.3 overs — the lowest Test total at Rawalpindi — before posting 325 for a 227-run lead. Sri Lanka fought back with 316 in their second innings (Milan Rathnayake 66 not out), leaving Pakistan 90 to win, which they reached in 13 overs for the loss of two wickets (Aamer Jamal 34 not out, Azan Awais 32 not out). Mohammad Abbas, who took 3-14 and 3-55, was Player of the Match.
