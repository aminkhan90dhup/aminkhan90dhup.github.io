Ayub Medical College — ETEA Project 873

Ayub Medical College, Abbottabad — a Medical Teaching Institution (MTI) under the MTI Act 2015 — is recruiting on a contract basis from candidates across Pakistan for chairperson, faculty and administrative posts. The application route differs by serial: only the Pre Auditor post (Serial 05) is applied for through ETEA, while the other serials use the college's own prescribed form route described in the advertisement.

Key facts

Organisation: Ayub Medical College (MTI-AMC), Ayub Teaching Hospital, Abbottabad

Testing agency: ETEA (for Serial 05 — Pre Auditor — only)

Project: Recruitment Project 873

Posts listed in the official advertisement: Chairpersons (1 each) for Anaesthesia, Medicine, Biochemistry and Forensic Medicine; Professor / Associate Professor / Assistant Professor faculty posts in Anaesthesia, Anatomy, Biochemistry, Cardiology, Community Medicine, Critical Care, Emergency Medicine, Endocrinology, ENT, Forensic Medicine, Electrophysiology, Interventional Radiology, Medicine, Nephrology, Paediatric Intensive Care, Chemical Pathology, Microbiology, Histopathology, Pharmacology, Physiology, Psychiatry, Pulmonology, Radiology, Surgery, Urology, Renal Transplant Surgery and Vitreoretinal Surgery; Manager Security (IPS-06) — 1 post; Assistant Manager Security (IPS-05) — 1 post; Pre Auditor (IPS-04) — 1 post

Tenure: Contract basis under the MTI Act 2015 and MTI-ATH rules; chairperson term period stated as three years, renewable subject to satisfactory performance

Domicile: All Pakistan

Education: Chairperson and faculty posts: MBBS or an equivalent degree recognised by PMDC with a higher qualification in the relevant specialty (such as FCPS, FRCA or MRCP) and faculty experience, as per MTI Policy Board Regulations; a PMDC eligibility certificate and valid PMDC registration are mandatory. Manager / Assistant Manager Security: a bachelor degree with armed forces or police service at the required rank and security management experience, or the civilian experience route stated in the advertisement. Pre Auditor: MBA (Finance), M.Com, CA (Inter), ICMAP (Inter) or ACCA (Inter) with 3 years of post-qualification experience; age up to 40 years

Online form available (ETEA route): 20 October 2026 on etea.edu.pk

Last date: 5 November 2026 on the ETEA projects list for the ETEA route (Serial 05). The advertisement text gives a 30 October 2026 deadline for submission of applications for the other serials — check the official advertisement for the serial you are applying for

How to apply

  1. Read the official advertisement carefully first and note the serial number of your post — the application route is different for Serial 05 and for Serials 01 to 04.
  2. For the Pre Auditor post (Serial 05) only: create your profile on the official ETEA website, print the E-Sahulat token, get your CNIC verified at a NADRA E-Sahulat franchise, apply online for Project 873, and deposit the test fee through OneLink.
  3. For Serials 01 to 04: use the prescribed form available on www.ayubmed.edu.pk or www.ath.gov.pk and submit your application electronically as a single PDF through the Google form / email route given in the advertisement, with the processing fee paid as directed there.
  4. Keep the original deposit slip / bank draft safe and do not share it with anyone.
  5. Only shortlisted candidates will be called for interview; bring original documents at the time of interview.

Hazrat's analysis

This is a senior academic and clinical opening at one of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's largest teaching institutions, with chairperson and faculty posts across a very wide range of specialties, plus a small number of security and audit posts. Because the ETEA route covers only the Pre Auditor post and the other posts follow the college's own form and deadline, the most common mistake would be applying through the wrong channel — candidates should confirm their serial's route and closing date in the official advertisement before paying any fee.
