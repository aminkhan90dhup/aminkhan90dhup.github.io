Free Chromebooks for Class 9 High Scorers — Prime Minister's Digital Pakistan Initiative

All principals and heads of government schools in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa are informed that Class 9 students who obtained 424 or more marks in the Science group or 315 or more marks in the Arts group (SSC Annual-I Examination 2026) are eligible for free Chromebooks under the Prime Minister's Digital Pakistan Initiative Programme.

BISE Peshawar has already submitted the data of eligible students to the Inter Boards Coordination Commission (IBCC), Islamabad. School heads must check the list of their institution's eligible students, fill in the official proforma with complete, accurate and verified information, and submit the duly signed hard copy to the Office of the Director Equivalence, IBCC, Plot No. 25, Street No. 38, G-10/4, Islamabad, with intimation to the Director Research & Development, BISE Peshawar.

Google DeepMind Uses AI to Prove Signal's Encryption Code Is Secure

Google DeepMind has announced a major breakthrough in software security. Its new system, AlphaProof Code, used teams of AI agents to mathematically prove key security properties of the real code behind the Signal messaging protocol — the technology that protects billions of private chats in Signal and Google Messages. The agents wrote about 6.5 million lines of machine-checked proof in just four weeks, a job DeepMind says would have taken expert teams years.

The proof covers the parts of Signal that set up a secure chat and keep changing its keys — checking that messages stay private, that old messages stay safe even if a key leaks, and that a chat can recover after a compromise. To test the system, researchers put two real, already-fixed bugs back into a test copy of the code — and in both cases AlphaProof Code proved the buggy version was unsafe. The work does not cover the whole Signal app, such as group chats, but DeepMind has published its proofs openly so other experts can check them.
