Important News — Education

Free Chromebooks for Class 9 High Scorers — Prime Minister's Digital Pakistan Initiative

All principals and heads of government schools in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa are informed that Class 9 students who obtained 424 or more marks in the Science group or 315 or more marks in the Arts group (SSC Annual-I Examination 2026) are eligible for free Chromebooks under the Prime Minister's Digital Pakistan Initiative Programme.

BISE Peshawar has already submitted the data of eligible students to the Inter Boards Coordination Commission (IBCC), Islamabad. School heads must check the list of their institution's eligible students, fill in the official proforma with complete, accurate and verified information, and submit the duly signed hard copy to the Office of the Director Equivalence, IBCC, Plot No. 25, Street No. 38, G-10/4, Islamabad, with intimation to the Director Research & Development, BISE Peshawar.

Google to Show News Links More Prominently in AI Search Results

Google announced on 15 October that links to websites and news articles will appear more often and higher up in its AI-powered search results. The change follows warnings from publishers and brands that AI-written summaries are sharply reducing the number of readers who visit the original websites.

The company will also expand its link carousel worldwide — cards with headlines and images shown below AI Overviews — and give priority to sources a reader has marked as preferred. Links at the end of AI summaries will be made clearer for people who want to read further. For students and teachers, the change is a reminder to open the original sources behind an AI answer, not just trust the summary. A Chartbeat report cited by AFP found publisher traffic from Google fell about 40 percent between July 2025 and July 2026.

More headlines: Apple's first foldable phone, the iPhone Duo, opened for pre-orders today with in-store availability set for 23 October, BusinessToday reports. Rokid has opened what it calls the world's first unmanned AI glasses experience store in Hong Kong, PR Newswire reports. RoboParty has launched RP1, an open-source humanoid robot for research into embodied AI, PR Newswire also reports. In India, ISRO has successfully conducted a hot test of a semi-cryogenic engine pre-burner for its next-generation launch vehicles, the Sunday Guardian Live reports.
