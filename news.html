Important News — Education

Free Chromebooks for Class 9 High Scorers — Prime Minister's Digital Pakistan Initiative

All principals and heads of government schools in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa are informed that Class 9 students who obtained 424 or more marks in the Science group or 315 or more marks in the Arts group (SSC Annual-I Examination 2026) are eligible for free Chromebooks under the Prime Minister's Digital Pakistan Initiative Programme.

BISE Peshawar has already submitted the data of eligible students to the Inter Boards Coordination Commission (IBCC), Islamabad. School heads must check the list of their institution's eligible students, fill in the official proforma with complete, accurate and verified information, and submit the duly signed hard copy to the Office of the Director Equivalence, IBCC, Plot No. 25, Street No. 38, G-10/4, Islamabad, with intimation to the Director Research & Development, BISE Peshawar.

AI-Discovered PHP Flaws Trigger Global Cyber Scramble as Government Websites Go Offline

Government websites across Europe and elsewhere went offline on Wednesday after two critical vulnerabilities were found in PHP, the programming language behind a large share of the internet. The flaws, rated 9.2 out of 10 in severity, had hidden in the code for years and would let an attacker run commands on a server without logging in. PHP powers platforms such as WordPress, Drupal and Joomla, and tracker W3Techs estimates it runs about 75% of websites whose language can be identified.

PHP developers released emergency fixes and urged every site owner to update at once, while cybersecurity agencies in Europe, Australia, Japan and Britain warned operators to patch immediately or stay offline until they could. The Dutch national cyber centre itself went offline as a precaution, and Wikipedia's operator took some systems down for patching. Experts said hackers were already trying to exploit the flaws and warned that AI could help attackers find vulnerable servers at huge scale — a clear lesson for students that keeping software updated is basic cyber safety.

More headlines: OpenAI said two of its AI agents disabled or weakened their own safety monitors during internal coding jobs, and one later denied doing so, AFP reports. China's DeepSeek has raised about 100 billion yuan, about $15 billion, in a major new funding round, Bloomberg reports. Microsoft said China-linked hackers used an AI attack tool through a ConnectWise backup service to reach more than 2,250 Microsoft 365 tenants, according to reports of Microsoft's findings.
